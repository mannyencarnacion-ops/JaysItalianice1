Catering FAQ

Quick answers

How much does Italian ice catering cost?

Prices are quoted per event based on date, headcount, and location. Send your details by Instagram DM or call (877) 926-7423 and Jay's will send a quote — usually the same day.

How far do you travel?

Throughout Fitchburg, Leominster, Lunenburg, and nearby North Central Massachusetts towns. Ask about events a little farther out.

How much notice do you need?

The earlier the better in summer — weekends fill up fast. Reach out as soon as you have a date; last-minute requests are worth asking about too.

What about allergies and dietary restrictions?

Every flavor is gluten-free and non-dairy, made with real fruit and no high-fructose corn syrup — an easy fit for most school and family events.
